# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Financially literate South African adults who understand RA, TFSA, pension funds, Monte Carlo simulations, and tax treatment. They open this tool to run projections, test scenarios, and verify numbers — not to be taught. They expect precision and control, and trust comes from correctness, not reassurance.

## Product Purpose

SA-specific retirement planning calculator. Runs deterministic projections and Monte Carlo simulations across multi-account portfolios (Pension Fund, RA, Preservation Fund, TFSA, Discretionary) with accurate SA tax treatment. Success looks like a user who can model their exact situation, stress-test assumptions, and walk away knowing whether their plan holds.

## Brand Personality

Sharp · Modern · Technical

The tool should feel like using Linear or Raycast in the finance domain: fast, precise, dark-first, zero bloat. The interface recedes so the data leads. Authority comes from accuracy, not from institutional decoration.

## Anti-references

- **Consumer fintech** (Mint, PocketSmith): cheerful palette, gamified savings nudges, soft rounded everything, pastel metric cards — too soft for serious planning
- **Generic SaaS dashboards**: navy-and-gold finance cliché, hero metrics with gradients, identical icon-card grids, tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section
- **Bank/insurance portals**: corporate beige, stiff layouts, excessive white space that wastes density, no keyboard power

## Design Principles

1. **Data density over decoration** — Financially literate users come for numbers. The interface should carry maximum information per pixel without feeling cluttered. Chrome recedes; projections lead.
2. **Precision over warmth** — Every number should feel exact. Trust is earned by correctness and SA-specific accuracy (right tax limits, right account types), not by reassuring copy or rounded values.
3. **Speed of thought** — Like Linear/Raycast: interactions feel instant. State changes are immediate. The tool responds to intent, not to multi-step wizards.
4. **South African specificity as feature** — RA, TFSA, pension fund rules, tax year limits — the specificity is what makes this useful. Never genericise or internationalise at the cost of SA accuracy.
5. **Restrained chrome, expressive data** — UI elements (sidebar, top bar, buttons) should be quiet and consistent. Visual weight belongs to charts and metric values, not to decorative surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1. Keyboard navigable. Focus rings visible. Both dark and light themes must independently meet AA. Color alone must never carry information in charts — use shape/pattern/label as well.

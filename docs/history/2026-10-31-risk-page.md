# 2026-10-31 — Risk page: Monte Carlo and volatility in their own section

## Why

The Monte Carlo success rate depends heavily on one volatility assumption: 70% at 16.5%, 93%
at 10% for the same plan. That assumption lived in Market Assumptions on the Plan page, and
the success rate was mixed into verdicts across Overview, Projections and Charts. The user
asked for Monte Carlo and its inputs to "live in its own section completely". Decided with
the user: **link only**. Every other page shows deterministic results, and Overview carries
one line linking to the section, which is named **Risk**.

## What moved

| From | To Risk |
|---|---|
| Plan → Market Assumptions: equity and bond volatility | `VolatilityForm` (`components/inputs/volatility-form.tsx`) |
| Charts: Monte Carlo fan chart, plan verdict, investment-scenarios chart | `MonteCarloChart`, verdict, `ScenarioComparisonChart` |
| Projections → Insights: Investment Scenarios card | `InvestmentScenariosCard` (`components/results/investment-scenarios-card.tsx`), titled "Scenario Details" |
| Projections → Calculations breakdown: Monte Carlo accordion | `MonteCarloMethodology` (`components/results/monte-carlo-methodology.tsx`), rewritten for the current model |
| Overview metrics: Plan Success Rate, simulated depletion | Three Risk cards: success rate, futures that fall short, typical shortfall age |

## What changed elsewhere

- **Simulation scope.** `calculator-context.tsx` runs the worker only on `/calculator/risk`.
  Previously it ran on Overview, Charts and Projections; the what-if panel ran two more
  workers and the insights panel ran `compareScenarios` (3 × 1,000 runs, main thread).
- **Overview.** The metrics grid is deterministic. "Portfolio Depletion" (from simulation
  thresholds) became "Income Lasts Until" (from the projection). The run-status strip became
  `RiskLink`, a no-numbers link. Key Insights' on-track badge uses the projection only.
- **Projections.** The summary's success block was removed. The what-if panel's success
  probability became a deterministic "Income lasts until" readout with its change against the
  current plan.
- **AI plan narrative.** `simulation` was removed from the payload, the request schema and the
  prompt. The prompt now states the figures assume steady returns.
- **Shared helper.** `computePortfolioTotals` (`lib/calculations/utils/portfolio-totals.ts`)
  replaces the copy in the Charts page and Insights panel, which Risk would have needed a
  third time.
- **Scenario comparison** is computed once on Risk and passed to both the chart and the card.
  Fixed in passing: the chart highlighted the recommended bar by comparing the display name
  ("Balanced") with the key ("balanced"), so the highlight never showed.
- **Navigation.** Risk was added to the sidebar, bottom nav (8 items fit at 390px), top-bar
  titles and command palette. CLAUDE.md records the routes and a rule against showing Monte
  Carlo outside Risk.

## Verification

- `npm run check` (0 errors), `npm run typecheck`, `npm run test` (all pass), build, `docs:check`.
- In the browser with a 3-account plan imported: Risk renders the verdict, the three result
  cards, the volatility inputs, the fan chart, the scenarios chart and details, and the
  methodology. Overview, Projections, Charts and Plan contain no "success rate", "success
  probability" or "Monte Carlo" text, and Plan has no volatility inputs. No console errors.
  No horizontal overflow at 390px.

## Not changed / open

- `components/dashboard/sticky-results-bar.tsx` still references the success rate, but it is
  not rendered anywhere (dead code, Phase 11).
- `e2e/journeys/*` (screenshot documentation, not run in CI) wait for "Success Rate" on
  Overview. They already targeted the pre-redesign tab layout.
- **The investment scenarios disagree with the plan's own success rate** (e.g. 97–98% vs 70%).
  `compareScenarios` pools everything into one discretionary account (no living annuity
  ceiling) with hard-coded returns of 10.5%, 12% and 14% and volatilities of 10%, 14% and 18%,
  ignoring the accounts' own returns. This predates the move; it became visible once both sat
  on one page. To be addressed with per-account investment types.
- Bond volatility is shown, but the simulation does not use it yet; its tooltip says so.
